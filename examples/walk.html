Truck yard

A truck idles in the middle of a 20 by 20 yard. You start in the southwest corner facing north. Walk around it and the truck moves through the whole field, including behind you. Headphones make the effect much clearer, because the sound is placed with HRTF.

Press start, then use the arrows to walk, q and e to turn, space to hear your position, and escape to stop. The arrows move relative to where you face, so up walks forward and down walks backward. The truck has a range box around it, so it stops moving once you are standing at it.
