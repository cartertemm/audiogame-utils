Audio menu example

Press start and music begins. Volume and Pan change it while it plays, so you hear the result as you move the slider. Loop restarts the track, because a sound takes its loop flag when playback begins.

Use the arrow keys. Up and down move, left and right change a value, enter chooses, escape leaves. Game options opens a second menu, and Back returns to this one. The music keeps playing through all of it.

On a touch screen, swipe left and right to move, up and down to change a value, double tap to choose, and tap with two fingers to leave.
