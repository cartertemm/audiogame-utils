Riverside venue

The truck yard walk again, except the world comes out of a file. maps/venue.json is 20 wide and 40 deep, split into a parking lot at the south end and a concert hall at the north end. A truck idles in the middle of the lot and the stage sits along the far wall of the hall.

Nothing about that layout is in the code. The map module answers three questions as you walk: which tile you stand on, so concrete footsteps turn to wood when you enter the hall; which zone you are in, which is spoken when you cross; and where the looping sounds are, which the sound pool places with HRTF. Headphones make it much clearer.

Press start, then use the arrows to walk, q and e to turn, space to hear your position, and escape to stop. The arrows move relative to where you face, so up walks forward and down walks backward.
